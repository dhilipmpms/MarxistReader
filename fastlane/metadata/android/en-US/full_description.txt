Marxist Reader is an offline-first Android application designed for reading Marxist and leftist content, articles, and e-books with primary support for Tamil and secondary support for English content.

<b>Key Features:</b>
• <b>Feed:</b> Browse latest articles and publications.
• <b>Article Reader:</b> Clean reading interface with categories, tags, read-time estimates, and Tamil font support.
• <b>EPUB Reader:</b> Built-in e-book reader with custom typography, sepia/dark themes, and Text-to-Speech (TTS) support.
• <b>Offline Support:</b> All content is cached locally using Room database; read saved articles anytime without network connectivity.
• <b>Background Sync:</b> Automatic background synchronization powered by WorkManager.
• <b>Bookmarks & Saved Content:</b> Save articles and books for offline access.
• <b>Multilingual UI:</b> Toggle seamlessly between Tamil and English interfaces.

<b>Privacy & Open Source:</b>
• 100% Free and Open Source software under GPL-3.0.
• No tracking, ads, or analytics data collection.
